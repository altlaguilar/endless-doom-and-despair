#Endless Delivery

##Repository

[https://github.com/altlaguilar/endless-doom-and-despair.git]

##Description
A 2D side scrolling platformer set inside a hospital overrun by zombies. The player must fight off waves of zombies using a gun while trying to survive for as long as possible. The game focuses on trying to last as long as possible, with no final level or ending other than the player's death.

##Features

2D Platformer Movement - The player will be able to move left and right, jump between platforms, and navigate different areas of the hospital.

Zombie Enemies - Zombies will continuously spawn throughout the hospital and attempt to attack the player. Zombies could have different movement speeds and health depending on different environments.

Combat System - The player will be able to attack zombies using a pistol. Each enemy defeated will contribute to the player's score.

Health System - The player will have a limited amount of health. Taking damage from zombies will reduce health, and the game will end when the player's health reaches zero.

Hospital Environment - The game will feature a 2D hospital setting with areas such as hallways, and emergency rooms. 


##Challenges

- Learning how to implement 2D platformer movement, including jumping, gravity, and collision.

- Developing zombie enemy AI that can detect, follow, and attack the player.

- Designing and creating 2D sprites, animations, and hospital environments that fit the game's theme.

- Implementing a scoring and high score system that saves the player's best performance.

##Outcomes

Ideal Outcome:

A fully playable 2D platformer with a hospital environment, animated player and zombie sprites and an endless survival system. The game will feature a scoring system and a high score leaderboard or saved personal best. Players can restart after dying and continuously try to improve their score.

Minimal Viable Outcome:

A functional 2D platformer featuring one playable character, a basic hospital environment, zombies that spawn and chase the player, a working combat system, and a health system. The game will end when the player dies with an option to restart.
